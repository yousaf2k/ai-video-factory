# Context: Horror Subgenres
## Supernatural Horror
- **Spectral Presence**: Ghostly figures, flickering lights, shifting objects.
- **Ritualism**: Ancient incantations, sigils, possessed items.
- **The Veil**: Moments where the boundary between worlds thins.

## Slasher Horror
- **The Stalker**: A silent, unstoppable physical threat (often masked).
- **The Chase**: Dynamic movement through claustrophobic corridors.
- **The Weapon**: The signature item of the killer (knife, axe, metal).

## Psychological Horror
- **Distortion**: Reality shifting, mirrored surfaces, unreliable memories.
- **Isolation**: Feeling of being watched while alone.
- **The Unseen**: The terror of what is hidden behind closed doors.

## Cosmic Horror
- **The Incomprehensible**: Tentacled monstrosities, infinite voids, nonsensical geometry.
- **Madness**: Characters losing theirs minds as they witness eldritch truths.
- **Indifference**: Smallness of humanity in the face of ancient gods.

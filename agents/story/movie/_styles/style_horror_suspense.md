# Style: Horror & Suspense
## Narrative Tone
- **Dread**: Maintain a constant sense of impending doom. Avoid over-explaining the threat.
- **Pacing**: Use short, staccato sentences for high-tension scenes. Use longer, atmospheric descriptions for the build-up.
- **Silence**: When writing narration, include gaps for [Silence] or [Ambient Horror Sounds] to let the tension breathe.

## Emotional Beats
- **Vulnerability**: Focus on the characters' isolation, fear, and lack of control.
- **The Uncanny**: Use descriptions of things that are "almost" normal but "slightly off."
- **Visceral Details**: Use sensory language: "the metallic tang of blood," "the rhythmic scraping of bone," "the heavy, wet breath."

## Scripting Guidelines
- **Suspense**: Use a "Show, Don't Tell" approach for the threat.
- **Narration**: Whispered, breathless, or deeply ominous tones are preferred.
- **Pacing**: A scene should feel "too quiet" right before the climax.

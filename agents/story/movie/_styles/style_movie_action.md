# Style: Action Movie Narrative
## Narrative Tone
- **Intensity**: High-octane, fast-paced, and larger than life.
- **Pacing**: Use short, powerful sentences for intense action or combat. Use sharp, rhythmic dialogue and clear, impactful narration.
- **Impact**: Focus on consequences: explosions, shattering glass, screaming tires, and physical struggle.

## Emotional Beats
- **Heroism**: Focus on a character's determination, resilience, and personal stakes.
- **Triumph**: The feeling of overcoming impossible odds.
- **The Payoff**: Satisfying moments of physical success or justice.

## Scripting Guidelines
- **Action Beats**: Describe scenes with dynamic movement verbs: "lunges," "careens," "shatters," "soars."
- **Narration**: Direct, powerful tones are preferred.
- **Dynamics**: Balance loud, chaotic action with quiet, tense moments of anticipation.

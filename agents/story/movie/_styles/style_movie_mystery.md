# Style: Mystery Movie Narrative
## Narrative Tone
- **Intensity**: Intriguing, meticulous, and atmospheric.
- **Pacing**: Step-by-step unravelling, building toward a grand deduction.
- **Impact**: Focus on small details, clues, and hidden meanings.

## Emotional Beats
- **Beat 1**: The baffling crime or event.
- **Beat 2**: The gathering of suspects and clues.
- **Beat 3**: The genius deduction.

## Scripting Guidelines
- **Action Beats**: Use inquisitive verbs: 'examines', 'deduces', 'observes'.
- **Narration**: Analytical, observant, and calculating.
- **Dynamics**: Slow observation followed by rapid sequences of realization.

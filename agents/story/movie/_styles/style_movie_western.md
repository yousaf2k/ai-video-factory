# Style: Western Movie Narrative
## Narrative Tone
- **Intensity**: Dusty, honorable, and lawless.
- **Pacing**: Slow, tense standoffs erupting into sudden, loud violence.
- **Impact**: Spurs clinking, wind howling, gun smoke, and whiskey.

## Emotional Beats
- **Beat 1**: The arrival of the stranger.
- **Beat 2**: The injustice of the land.
- **Beat 3**: The high noon duel.

## Scripting Guidelines
- **Action Beats**: Use rugged verbs: 'draws', 'spits', 'gallops'.
- **Narration**: Gravelly, sparse, and mythical.
- **Dynamics**: Vast quiet landscapes broken by the crack of a revolver.

# Style: High Fantasy Movie Narrative
## Narrative Tone
- **Intensity**: Epic, magical, and mythic.
- **Pacing**: Grand sweeping journeys mixed with frantic magical battles.
- **Impact**: Spells casting, sword clashes, mythical beasts roaring.

## Emotional Beats
- **Beat 1**: The call to adventure and heroic destiny.
- **Beat 2**: Wonder at magical realms.
- **Beat 3**: The eternal struggle between light and darkness.

## Scripting Guidelines
- **Action Beats**: Use majestic verbs: 'conjures', 'soars', 'smites'.
- **Narration**: Grand, poetic, and legendary.
- **Dynamics**: Contrast peaceful magical glades with dark, corrupting forces.

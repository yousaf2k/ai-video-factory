# Style: Comedy Movie Narrative
## Narrative Tone
- **Intensity**: Lighthearted, energetic, and absurd.
- **Pacing**: Fast-paced banter, comedic timing, and visual gags.
- **Impact**: Slapstick falls, exaggerated reactions, witty retorts.

## Emotional Beats
- **Beat 1**: The absurd misunderstanding.
- **Beat 2**: The escalation of ridiculous stakes.
- **Beat 3**: The hilarious and heartwarming resolution.

## Scripting Guidelines
- **Action Beats**: Use snappy verbs: 'stumbles', 'quips', 'bumbles'.
- **Narration**: Witty, sarcastic, or playfully unreliable.
- **Dynamics**: Build tension solely to break it with a punchline.

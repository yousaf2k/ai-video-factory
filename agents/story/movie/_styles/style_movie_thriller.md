# Style: Thriller Movie Narrative
## Narrative Tone
- **Intensity**: Tense, suspenseful, and psychological.
- **Pacing**: Slow burns punctuated by sudden, shocking twists.
- **Impact**: Paranoia, racing hearts, shadows, and creeping dread.

## Emotional Beats
- **Beat 1**: The creeping realization that something is wrong.
- **Beat 2**: The desperation of being hunted or framed.
- **Beat 3**: The shocking revelation of the truth.

## Scripting Guidelines
- **Action Beats**: Use tense verbs: 'lurks', 'gasps', 'sprints'.
- **Narration**: Unreliable, paranoid, and breathless.
- **Dynamics**: Sustained tension with minimal release until the very end.

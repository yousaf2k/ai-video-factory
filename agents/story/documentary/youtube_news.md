## Video Objective
Generate a fast-paced, sensational news story tailored for the YouTube Shorts platform (typically 60 to 120 seconds). The narrative should hook the viewer instantly, deliver breaking news or shocking facts rapidly, and maintain high retention.

## Visual Style

- **Sensational & Urgent**: Use breaking news aesthetics, flashing graphics, and high-energy transitions.
- **Fast-Paced Delivery**: Keeps the viewer hooked with rapid information flow tailored for the Shorts format.
- **Bold Text Overlays**: Highlight key facts, quotes, or shocking figures directly on screen.
- **Eye-Catching Imagery**: Focus on dramatic, saturated, and highly engaging focal points.
- **Dynamic Camera Moves**: Constant zooms, pans, and kinetic energy to maintain retention.

## Shorts Story Structure

- **The Hook (First 3-5 seconds)**: Unmissable breaking news alert, immediately presenting the core sensational fact or question.
- **The Core Story**: Rapid-fire details, using controversy, shock value, or extreme intrigue to keep viewers watching.
- **The Climax**: The most shocking revelation, implication, or twist in the news story.
- **The Outro**: Quick ending asking viewers for their opinion, to subscribe, or pushing engagement.

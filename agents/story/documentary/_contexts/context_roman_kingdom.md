# Context: The Roman Kingdom (c. 753-509 BCE)

## Era Overview
The legendary and formative period of Rome’s history, prior to the Republic. A time of seven kings, Etruscan influence, and the unification of the Seven Hills from primitive settlements into a city-state.

## Historical Facts & Timeline

### The Seven Kings (Traditional)
- **Romulus (753 BCE)**: The founding king; plowman of the *Pomerium* (sacred boundary).
- **Numa Pompilius**: The religious reformer; established priesthoods and the 12-month calendar.
- **Tullus Hostilius**: The warrior king; destroyed Alba Longa.
- **Ancus Marcius**: Established Ostia and the first bridge (Pons Sublicius).
- **Tarquinius Priscus**: The first Etruscan king; built the Circus Maximus and the Cloaca Maxima.
- **Servius Tullius**: Reformed the army and built the first defensive wall (Servian Wall).
- **Tarquinius Superbus (535-509 BCE)**: The tyrannical final king whose downfall birthed the Republic.

### Key Innovations & Events
- **Etruscan Governance**: Early Roman culture was heavily shaped by Etruscan architects and kings.
- **Cloaca Maxima**: The draining of the Forum Romanum marsh, enabling central urban life.
- **Treaty with Carthage (509 BCE)**: Defining Rome's early external status.

## High-Fidelity Sensory Details
- **Visuals**: Primitive wattle-and-daub huts on the Palatine Hill, thatched roofs with cooking fires, early timber temples with brightly painted terracotta decorations, dirt paths between wooden shops.
- **Sounds**: The clatter of early infantry (Greek-style phalanx), the crackle of wood fires, the sounds of archaic Latin and Etruscan.
- **Smells**: Smoke from charcoal braziers, the salty Tiber air, the dampness of the drying Forum marshes.

## Authentic Vocabulary
- *Pomerium* (Sacred Boundary), *Regia* (Royal Palace), *Curia* (Senate House), *Lictors* (King's Attendants), *Toga/Tunics* (Archaic Clothing).

## Anachronism Guardrails
- ❌ No Marble Architecture (No Parthenon/Colosseum-style buildings; this is timber and tufa).
- ❌ No Lorica Segmentata (Early soldiers used bronze breastplates and round shields).
- ❌ No concrete (Pre-3rd century BCE).
- ❌ No Imperial titles (These are Kings, not Emperors).
- ❌ No Christianity (Pre-birth of Jesus).

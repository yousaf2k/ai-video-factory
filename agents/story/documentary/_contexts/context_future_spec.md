# Context: Speculative Future (2030-2200+)

## Era Overview
The trajectory of human and post-human evolution, spanning near-future Earth to far-future interstellar civilizations. A world where biology and technology blur.

## Historical Facts & Timeline (Plausible Future)

### Near-Future (2030-2050): The Spark
- **AI Singularity**: The rise of AGI (Artificial General Intelligence) and its integration into global infrastructure.
- **Cybernetics**: Widespread neural implants, lab-grown organs, and autonomous urban landscapes.
- **Climate Integration**: Cities redesigned for environmental restoration and carbon-negative living.

### Mid-Future (2060-2100): The Expansion
- **Fusion Power**: Post-scarcity energy economy via stable nuclear fusion.
- **Off-World**: Permanent lunar and Martian colonies, space elevators, and asteroid mining.
- **Planetary Restoration**: Large-scale terraforming initiatives to heal Earth's biosphere.

### Far-Future (2100-2200+): The Transcendence
- **Deep Space**: Photon-driven generation ships, Dyson swarms, and first contact with extraterrestrial intelligence.
- **Transhumanism**: Mind uploading, digital immortality, and post-human evolution into non-biological forms.
- **Reality Editing**: Mastery over gravitational waves, quantum computing at scale, and simulated universes.

## High-Fidelity Sensory Details
- **Visuals**: Neon-lit bio-luminescent skyscrapers, holographic dragons in the rain, metallic sheen of solar sails, the hum of a data center spanning an entire city block.
- **Sounds**: The rhythmic pulse of a fusion core, the silent glide of mag-lev trains, the multi-layered whispers of a global neural net.
- **Smells**: Ozone and rain-slicked pavement, the sterile scent of an orbital laboratory, the lush synthetic biosphere of a generation ship.

## Authentic Vocabulary
- *AGI* (Artificial General Intelligence), *Neural Net* (Brain Interconnect), *Post-Scarcity* (Economics), *Interstellar* (Travel), *Cybernetic* (Hardware).

## Anachronism Guardrails
- ❌ No "Magic" (Stay grounded in plausible science/physics).
- ❌ No generic "lasers and spaceships" (Provide depth/functioning logic).
- ❌ No Utopian-only views (Future civilizations have political and social fallout from tech too).
- ❌ No modern-day limitations (Acknowledge that today's problems might be solved, while and new ones arise).

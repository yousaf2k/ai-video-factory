# Context: Greek Hellenistic Period (323-31 BCE)

## Era Overview
The era following Alexander the Great's death, marked by the transformation of the Greek world into vast international kingdoms, cultural fusion (Hellenization), and extraordinary scientific and mathematical advancements.

## Historical Facts & Timeline

### Successor Wars (323-301 BCE)
- **Babylon (323 BCE)**: Death of Alexander; his empire is carved up by his generals (Diadochi).
- **Ptolemy I**: Seizes Egypt, founding the Ptolemaic dynasty.
- **Seleucus I**: Claims the vast eastern territories (Seleucid Empire).
- **Battle of Ipsus (301 BCE)**: Final consolidation of the four major Hellenistic kingdoms.

### Golden Age of Alexandria (Ptolemaic Egypt)
- **Library of Alexandria**: The world's largest repository of knowledge; 500,000+ scrolls.
- **Pharos Lighthouse**: Built in 280 BCE; one of the Seven Wonders of the Ancient World.
- **Museum**: A research institute supporting scholars like Euclid and Hero.

### Scientific & Philosophical Milestones
- **Euclid**: *Elements* (Geometry foundation).
- **Archimedes**: Physics, buoyancy, and the defense of Syracuse.
- **Eratosthenes**: Calculating the Earth's circumference.
- **New Philosophies**: Stoicism (Zeno), Epicureanism (Epicurus), and Skepticism (Pyrrho).
- **Cleopatra VII**: The last active ruler of the Ptolemaic Kingdom (30 BCE).

### The Coming of Rome
- **Macedonian Wars**: Rome defeats and annexes Macedonia (168 BCE).
- **Battle of Actium (31 BCE)**: Octavian's victory over Mark Antony and Cleopatra, ending Hellenistic independence.

## Cultural Knowledge & Art
- **Hellenization**: The spread of Greek language, architecture, and administration across the Near East.
- **Artistic Realism**: Moving away from Classical restraint into deep emotional expression (e.g., *Laocoön Group*, *Winged Victory of Samothrace*, *Dying Gaul*).
- **Multicultural Hubs**: Cities like Antioch and Alexandria where Greek, Egyptian, Hebrew, and Persian cultures intersected.

## High-Fidelity Sensory Details
- **Visuals**: The monumental Pharos lighthouse beacon at night, grand papyrus-filled library alcoves, mosaic-floored palaces, multicultural crowds in Antioch.
- **Sounds**: A polyglot of languages in the harbor, the grinding of Archimedes' mechanical gears, the rustle of papyrus scrolls.
- **Smells**: Spices from the Far East, the scent of expensive Egyptian linen, the bustle of international maritime trade.

## Authentic Vocabulary
- *Alexandria/Antioch* (Major Cities), *Successor* (Ruler Type), *Scholar* (Cultural Hero), *Multiculturalism* (Societal Context).

# Context: Green Snake Spirit Realm
## World Building
- **Environment**: Chinese xianxia fantasy worlds — mist-shrouded karst mountains, lotus ponds, ancient pavilions with sweeping curved roofs, lantern-lit streets, and ornate temple courtyards. Ruined spirit cities of blackened wood and stone, battered by perpetual windstorms, streaked with fire and glowing debris.
- **Scale**: Grand and mythic — towering gateways, colossal statues, and vast spirit realms dwarfing the characters; intimate moments framed against endless painted horizons.
- **Atmosphere**: Mystical and fateful. Layers of mist and ink-wash haze, floating petals and embers, drifting spirit lights, skies that shift between serene daylight and storm-lit supernatural tumult.

## Subject Integration
- **Interaction**: Characters move through the world with martial grace — silk robes brushing water surfaces, hands trailing glowing energy, sword strokes splitting rain or mist. The environment reacts: lanterns flare, petals scatter, ink swirls in their wake.
- **Emotion**: Fierce loyalty, longing, and defiance. Compositions alternate between tender intimacy and epic solitude; even quiet moments carry an undercurrent of supernatural destiny.

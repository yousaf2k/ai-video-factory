# Visual Context: Greek Archaic Period (c. 800-490 BCE)
## Chronologically Specific Visuals
- **Sculpture**: Kouros (standing nude male) and Kore (draped female) statues, frontal rigid poses, almond-shaped eyes, braided hair, "Archaic Smile," painted detailing.
- **Pottery**: Black-figure style (black silhouettes on red clay, incised detail). **NO Red-figure**.
- **Architecture**: Early stone temples (Temple of Apollo at Corinth), heavy Doric columns, no entasis yet, simple capitals, mud-brick and timber house walls.

## Military (Early Hoplite)
- **Armor**: Bronze Corinthian-style helmets, muscle cuirasses, round aspis shields with painted blazons (owls, gorgons).
- **Tactics**: Early phalanx formations, heavy iron spears, short xiphos swords.

## Social & Urban
- **Sanctuaries**: Developing Delphi and Olympia; early stone treasuries, metal tripods (bronze).
- **Polis**: Emerging grid streets (Hippodamian later), early agoras with simple stoas.

## Art & Ornament
- **Motifs**: Orientalizing (lotus, griffins), Geometric meanders (early archaic).
- **Writing**: Early Greek alphabet inscriptions on stone stelai and pottery.

## Style Guardrails
- **Show Transition**: A civilization in its secondary rebirth; more experimental than classical.
- **STRICTLY NO**: Parthenon (Classical), Naturalistic Hellenistic realism, Red-figure pottery.

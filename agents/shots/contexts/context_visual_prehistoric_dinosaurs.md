# Visual Context: Prehistoric Dinosaurs (Mesozoic Era)
## Era-Specific Ecosystems
- **Cretaceous Period**: Lush fern and cycad forests, swampy wetlands with cypress-like trees (draped in moss), tidal flats, volcanic landscapes with active geology.
- **Jurassic Period**: Dense conifer forests, Araucaria (Monkey Puzzle) trees, ancient pines, vast semi-arid plains, oxbow lakes.
- **Triassic Period**: Arid deserts, dunes, early cone-bearing forests, rugged Pangaea landscapes.

## Creature Visuals (Flux2.Dev Guidelines)
- **Theropods (e.g., T-Rex)**: Slit pupils, serrated 6-inch teeth, powerful muscular builds, dark green/brown scales with subtle patterns, protofeathers on arms (Maiasaura).
- **Sauropods (e.g., Brachiosaurus)**: 80-foot tall silhouettes, legs like ancient tree trunks, elephantine skin textures.
- **Ceratopsians (e.g., Triceratops)**: Three-horned heads, massive bony frills, beaked mouths, thick pebbled skin.
- **Flyers (e.g., Pterosaurs)**: Leathery wing membranes, soaring above landscapes.

## Environmental & Atmospheric
- **Atmospheric Effects**: Volumetric fog, mist rising from stagnant water, heavy dust trails from migrating herds, trembling ground effects.
- **Flora**: Giant ferns, cycads, ginkgoes, ancient conifers, Spanish moss.
- **Geology**: Volcanic mountain ranges, active lava flows (Triassic/Cretaceous), river deltas.

## Lighting & Color
- **Lighting**: Dappled sunlight through canopy, sunbeams (god rays), volcanic glow, dramatic storm clouds with lightning.
- **Palette**: Rich jungle greens, earthy browns, terracotta, volcanic orange/yellow, storm grays, golden hour warmth.

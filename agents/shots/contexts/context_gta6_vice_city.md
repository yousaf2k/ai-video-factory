# Visual Context: GTA 6 - Vice City (Leonida)
## Visual Archetypes
- **Vice City Glamour**: Ultra-luxury waterfront mansions, pastel Art Deco hotels, neon-drenched Ocean Drive, high-end supercars, palm-lined boulevards.
- **Gritty Everglades**: Remote swampy marshlands, airboats, muddy backwaters, sun-distressed trailers, dense mangrove forests, humid atmosphere.
- **Urban Chaos**: Bustling downtown streets, highway interchanges, colorful graffiti, crowded sidewalks, flashy storefronts, police activity.
- **Beach Life**: Sun-bleached sand, turquoise ocean water, jet skis, crowded beach clubs, vibrant swimwear, midday heat haze.

## Culture & Atmosphere
- **The Hustle**: Underworld dealings, high-stakes robberies, fast cars, luxury fashion, gold chains, flashy jewelry.
- **Social Media Era**: Vertical video influence, viral street moments, smartphone photography vibe, modern viral culture.
- **Florida Gothic**: Eccentric local characters, wild animals (alligators), chaotic street life, "Leonida Man" energy.

## Atmospheric & Lighting
- **Golden Hour**: Intense orange and purple sunsets, long dramatic shadows, sun-kissed skin, deep blue ocean gradients.
- **Tropical Storm**: Sudden heavy downpours, slick wet asphalt, dramatic lightning, grey-cast humid sky, wind-blown palm trees.
- **Night Glow**: Vibrant pink/cyan/yellow neon reflections on wet streets, club lights, city skyline glow against dark indigo sky.

## Style Guardrails
- **Atmosphere**: Vibrant, humid, high-energy, cinematic yet grounded in modern realism.
- **STRICTLY NO**: Medieval tech, high-fantasy magic, futuristic hover-cars, sci-fi HUDs (unless parodying modern tech).

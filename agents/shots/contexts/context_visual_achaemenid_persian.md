# Visual Context: Achaemenid Persian Empire (550-330 BCE)
## Monumental Architecture
- **Persepolis (Parsa)**: The Apadana (audience hall) with 20m fluted columns and double-bull or griffin capitals. Gate of All Nations with colossal winged Lamassu (bull-men).
- **Materials**: Polished black limestone, cedar wood beams, polychrome glazed brick friezes (striding lions, archers in turquoise/yellow).
- **Pasargadae**: Tomb of Cyrus (stepped pyramid), four-fold Chahar Bagh gardens.
- **Naqsh-e Rustam**: Rock-cut royal tombs in the shape of massive crosses.

## Clothing & Court Culture
- **Royal**: Pleated long robes (Candys) in Tyrian purple and gold. Tall kidaris tiara. Golden torcs and armbands with animal terminals. Long, Curled, perfumed square beards.
- **Median/Persian Dress**: Trousers (anaxyrides), long-sleeved tunics, felt caps (basbash) covering the mouth. Vibrant patterns (rosettes, lions).
- **Vessels**: Gold/silver Rhytons (horn-shaped, animal heads), shallow Phialae bowls.

## Military & Religion
- **The Immortals**: spears with golden/silver apple counterweights, wicker screens (Spara shields), composite bows in Gorytos quivers. Scale armor under robes.
- **Zoroastrianism**: Ahura Mazda (Faravahar/winged disc symbol), open-air fire altars, Magi wearing mouth-covering hoods.

## Style Guardrails
- **Atmosphere**: Imperial splendor, organizational genius, plateau sunlight, high dignity.
- **STRICTLY NO**: Islamic architecture (mosques/minarets), scimitars (use straight Akinakes), turbans, or Roman elements.

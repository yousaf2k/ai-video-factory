# Visual Context: Greek Dark Ages (c. 1100-800 BCE)
## Chronologically Specific Visuals
- **Settlements**: Small hilltop refuge villages, mud-brick and wattle-and-daub houses, thatched roofs (straw/reed), stone foundations (field stones). NO monumental architecture.
- **Pottery**: Proto-Geometric (circles/semicircles) and Geometric (meanders, triangles) styles. Handmade feel, dark-on-light.
- **Technology**: Early iron working (swords, tools), bronze still used for elite tripods and pins.
- **Ruins**: People living among crumbling, roofless Mycenaean palace ruins (cyclopean walls, weeds growing through floors).

## Clothing & Appearance
- **Garments**: Simple rectangular cloth pinned with bronze fibulae (early peplos), wool chiton-like tunics.
- **Grooming**: Weathered, unrefined, practical for rural survival.
- **Jewelry**: Simple bronze pins, basic armlets, no elaborate goldwork (mostly).

## Social & Military
- **Social**: Tribal chieftains (basileis), small gatherers, oral storytellers (rhapsodes/bard-like).
- **Military**: Individual warriors with iron swords, spears, bronze conical helmets, round leather/bronze shields. **NO Phalanx**.

## Style Guardrails
- **Atmosphere**: Survival, simplicity, resilience, "mystery of the past" feeling.
- **STRICTLY NO**: Doric/Ionic temples, Marble sculpture (Kouros), Red-figure pottery, Democracy.

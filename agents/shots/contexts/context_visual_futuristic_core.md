# Visual Context: Futuristic Core (Sci-Fi & Speculative)
## Visual Archetypes
- **Cyberpunk (Neon-Noir)**: High-tech low-life. Rain-slicked streets, cyan/magenta neon, holographic ads, massive corporate towers, dense urban sprawl.
- **Solarpunk (Eco-Utopia)**: Harmony of nature and tech. Skyscraper-integrated vertical gardens, white/glass architecture, flowing organic shapes, solar-glass panels, clean water/air.
- **Chrome / Minimalist**: Functional "Apple" aesthetic. Brushed metal, white plastic, clean lines, minimalist space station interiors, polished surfaces.
- **Bio-Tech**: Fusion of biology and machinery. Pulsing circuity in trees, glowing veins in synthetic organisms, organic-growth buildings.

## Technology & Infrastructure
- **Interactions**: Holographic HUDs, neural brain-computer interfaces (glowing implants), augmented reality (labels over reality).
- **Megastructures**: Arcologies (city-in-a-building), orbital ringworlds, Dyson sphere segments, lunar/Martian domed colonies, underwater habitats.
- **Mobility**: Sleek hover-vehicles, vertical take-off craft (VTOL), maglev trains, personal jetpacks.
- **Robotics**: Humanoid androids (visible synth-skin/joints), maintenance drones, massive industrial mechs.

## Atmospheric & Lighting
- **Aesthetics**: Volumetric lighting (laser beams), bioluminescence (glowing flora/fauna), lens flares from ion engines.
- **Effects**: Atmospheric scattering (smog in cyberpunk, crystal clear in solarpunk), "Glitch" aesthetics (chromatic aberration for digital errors).

## Style Guardrails
- **Atmosphere**: Awe-inspiring or cautionary, depending on sub-genre.
- **STRICTLY NO**: Anachronistic early 21st-century tech (box computers, wires), combustion engines (unless in post-apoc context).

# Visual Context: Roman Kingdom / Regal Period (753-509 BCE)
## Chronologically Specific Visuals
- **Architecture**: Primitive hut dwellings (oval/rectangular), wattle-and-daub walls, thatched roofs (straw/reed). Transitioning to terracotta tiles and mud-brick under Etruscan influence.
- **Foundations**: Tufa and cappellaccio (local volcanic stone). NO marble, NO concrete.
- **Temples**: Etruscan-style (high podium, deep front porch, timber columns, terracotta statues/acroteria).
- **Infrastructure**: The Roman Forum as a drained marsh (muddy/gravelly), the Cloaca Maxima (stone arch drain under construction).

## Clothing & Appearance
- **Garments**: Early shorter Toga (simpler drape), wool tunics, leather belts.
- **Regal**: Toga praetexta (purple border), Trabea (all purple), ivory scepter, curule chair.
- **Military**: Phalanx formation (Greek/hoplite influence). Round bronze shields (aspis/clipeus), bronze breastplates, Corinthian/Italic bronze helmets. **Stricly NO imperial lorica segmentata**.

## Social & Technology
- **Setting**: The Seven Hills of Rome (Palatine, Capitoline, etc.) with scattered house clusters.
- **Technology**: Iron and bronze tools, handmade pottery (Impasto), later black Bucchero ware. NO coins (use Aes Rude - bronze scraps).

## Style Guardrails
- **Atmosphere**: Frontier energy, archaic solemnity, developing urbanism.
- **STRICTLY NO**: Marble temples, Colosseum, Pantheon, imperial legions, Latin inscriptions in classical script.

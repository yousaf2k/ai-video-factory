# Visual Context: Plague of Athens (430-426 BCE)
## Chronological & Event Context
- **Setting**: Classical Athens during the Peloponnesian War. Overcrowded conditions behind the Long Walls (refugees in temporary huts/temples).
- **Atmosphere**: Somber, desperate, "smoke-shrouded" city, moral collapse, lawlessness.

## Symptom Details (Thucydides' Account)
- **Early Stage**: Inflammation of the eyes, red/bloody throat/tongue, foul breath, sneezing, hoarseness.
- **Progression**: Violent coughing, retching, bilious vomiting, skin appearing reddish/livid with small blisters/ulcers.
- **Late Stage**: Extreme thirst (sunken eyes, dehydrated skin), gangrene in extremities (fingers, toes), emaciation.

## Social Crisis Visuals
- **Burial**: Abandoned traditional rites, bodies piled in streets, multiple funeral pyres burning simultaneously, mass graves (cist/pit).
- **Refugees**: Families living in temple porticos/public buildings, goatskin tents, scattered belongings.
- **Figures**: Pericles (aged leader) appearing gaunt/ill in late 429 BCE.

## Style Guardrails
- **Atmosphere**: Tragic, respectful, documentary-style, "sunlight filtering through smoke."
- **STRICTLY NO**: Horror-movie gore, modern medical gear, anachronistic Roman burials.

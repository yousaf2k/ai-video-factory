# Context: Visual Horror Elements
## Decayed Environments
- **Location 1**: Abandoned Asylum (peeling lead paint, rusty stretchers, flickering fluorescent lights).
- **Location 2**: Fogbound Forest (skeletal trees, tangled briars, low mist hiding feet).
- **Location 3**: Victorian Manor (dusty velvet, creaking floorboards, portraits with shifting eyes).

## Unsettling Biology
- **Features**: Elongated limbs, pale translucent skin, milky eyes, jagged serrated teeth.
- **Form**: Distorted silhouettes, shadows that move independently, twitching parasitic movements.

## Visceral Textures
- **Materials**: Wet leather, rusty metal, dried blood, slimy ichor, cracked porcelain.
- **Atmospheric**: Thick volumetric smoke, swirling ash, dense static grain.

## Horror Lighting Motifs
- **Flicker**: Rhythmic strobing lights to create disorienting frames.
- **Void**: Pitch-black silhouettes on high-contrast backgrounds.
- **Red/Green**: Sickly neon or traditional dramatic deep reds.

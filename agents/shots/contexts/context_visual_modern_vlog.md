# Visual Context: Modern Lifestyle Vlog
## Environments
- **Trendy Cafes**: Minimalist decor, hanging plants, wooden tables, ceramic mugs, espresso machines, large windows with natural light.
- **Urban Streets**: Brick walls, graffiti murals, clean asphalt, modern storefronts, pedestrians in background (soft-focus), street lamps.
- **Modern Interiors**: Contemporary furniture, pendant lighting, clean lines, cozy textures (pillows, rugs).
- **Outdoor Markets**: Colorful awnings, vibrant product displays (produce, crafts), bustling crowds.

## Clothing & Appearance
- **Contemporary Casual**: T-shirts, hoodies, denim, stylish jackets, beanies, sunglasses.
- **Creator Gear**: Smartwatches, backpacks, small microphones (clip-on), occasionally holding a sleek smartphone or gimbal.
- **Grooming**: Natural, well-kept, modern hairstyles, expressive facial expressions.

## Lighting & Color
- **Lighting**: Warm indoor ambient light, high-contrast natural daylight, golden hour warmth, soft overcast diffusion.
- **Palette**: Vibrant but natural color science; true-to-life tones, no heavy filters, authentic "action camera" color rendering.
- **Atmosphere**: Clean, energetic, spontaneous.

# Context: 3D Animated World
## World Building
- **Environment**: Environments should feel lived-in and rich with storytelling details. Whether it's a messy child's bedroom, a bustling city of monsters, or a cosmic landscape, it must have depth and "charm".
- **Scale**: Pay attention to the scale of the world relative to the characters. 
- **Atmosphere**: Use volumetric lighting (god rays) and atmospheric perspective to give depth to the 3D space.

## Subject Integration
- **Interaction**: Characters should interact believably with their environment. Their weight should be felt on the ground, and their shadows should anchor them.
- **Emotion**: The environment's lighting and color should actively reflect the character's internal emotional state.

# Visual Context: Greek Classical Period (c. 490-323 BCE)
## Architecture & Urbanism
- **Athens Acropolis**: Parthenon (Doric), Propylaea (gateway), Erechtheion (Ionic, Caryatid porch), Temple of Athena Nike.
- **Materials**: Pentelic marble (white/honey), Parian marble, limestone.
- **Public Spaces**: Pnyx Hill (assembly), Agora (market/politics), Theater of Dionysus.
- **Harbor**: Piraeus (trireme sheds, fortifications).
- **Other Sites**: Olympia (Temple of Zeus), Delphi (Sanctuary of Apollo), Pella (Macedonian pebble mosaics).

## Clothing & Adornment
- **Men**: Chiton (knee-length linen), Himation (woolen cloak), Chlamys (short traveler's cloak), Petasos (felt hat).
- **Women**: Peplos (heavy wool, pinned), Chiton (fine linen, ankle-length), Himation (shawl/veil).
- **Footwear**: Leather sandals, buskins (boots), or barefoot.
- **Grooming**: Trimmed beards (early), shaved (late); elaborate hair styling with ribbons.

## Military & Warfare (Hoplite Panoply)
- **Helmet**: Corinthian (classic bronze), Attic (no nose guard), Pilos (Spartan conical).
- **Cuirass**: Linothorax (linen/leather), Muscle cuirass (bronze), Xiphos (short sword), Kopis (curved).
- **Shield**: Aspis (round bronze, ~1m), blazons (Owl, Lambda).
- **Macedonian**: Sarissa (6m pike), Companion cavalry with white chlamys.

## Interior & Material Culture
- **Pottery**: Red-figure (black ground, red figures), White-ground lekythoi (funerary).
- **Sculpture**: Polykleitos (contrapposto), Phidias (ivory/gold), Praxiteles (soft flesh/marble).
- **Furniture**: Klinai (couches), Ostraka (pottery shards for voting), papyrus rolls.

## Environmental & Lighting
- **Atmosphere**: Mediterranean sun, rocky hills, olive groves, dusty roads.
- **Colors**: Mediterranean blue, Pentelic white, terracotta, bronze patina, Spartan red.
- **Lighting**: Greek golden hour, harsh midday contrasts.

# Visual Context: Greek Hellenistic Period (323-31 BCE)
## Chronologically Specific Visuals
- **Architecture**: Monumental scale, Corinthian order (acanthus leaves), ornate detailing, Pharos Lighthouse (triple-tier), Library of Alexandria (scroll-filled shelves).
- **Sculpture**: "Hellenistic Baroque" - high emotional realism, dramatic poses, twisting forms (Laocoön), wind-swept drapery (Winged Victory). Naturalistic portraits of old age and diverse ethnicities.
- **Urbanismo**: Hippodamian grids, massive stoas, wide colonnaded streets, theaters for thousands.
- **Pottery**: West Slope ware, Terra Sigillata (red glossy relief). **Painted figured pottery fades**.

## Cultural Fusion (Syncretism)
- **Settings**: Alexandria (Egypt), Antioch (Syria), Pergamon (Anatolia), Seleucia (Mesopotamia).
- **People**: Multi-ethnic crowds (Greeks, Egyptians, Persians, Jews), Greek-style dress with Eastern embroidery/trousers.
- **Religion**: Fusion of gods (Serapis, Isis-Aphrodite), colossal temples.

## Science & Military
- **Science**: Scholars with papyrus scrolls, armillary spheres, water clocks, engineering models (Archimedes).
- **Military**: Macedonian phalanx with sarissas (6m pikes), war elephants (with towers), multi-banked galleys (quinqueremes).

## Style Guardrails
- **Atmosphere**: Cosmopolitan, opulent, intellectual, international.
- **STRICTLY NO**: Archaic smiles, rigid poses, simple village life, Geometric pottery.

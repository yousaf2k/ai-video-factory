# Visual Context: Ancient Greece (Core Essentials)
## Universal Geography & Climate
- **Landscape**: Rugged limestone mountains, rocky coastlines, deep blue Aegean/Ionian seas, pebble beaches, olive groves, vineyards.
- **Climate**: Intense Mediterranean sunlight, harsh midday shadows, golden hour warmth, clear blue skies (occasionally storm clouds).
- **Vegetation**: Olive trees (silver-green leaves), cypress, pines, grapevines, laurel, oleander, wild thyme.

## Core Clothing (Universal Basics)
- **Materials**: Primarily linen (summer) and wool (winter).
- **Men**: Chiton (tunic), Himation (large cloak), sandals or barefoot.
- **Women**: Peplos (pinned wool), Chiton (belted linen/silk), Himation (shawl/veil).
- **Colors**: Natural whites/creams, saffron yellow, murex purple (wealthy), madder red, indigo blue.

## Material Culture
- **Pottery**: Amphorae (storage), Kraters (mixing), Kylikes (drinking).
- **Domestic**: Flat-roofed mud-brick houses, central courtyards, clay tile roofs (mostly).
- **Lighting**: Terracotta oil lamps, hearth fires, torches.

## Visual Polish
- **Atmosphere**: Dust motes in sunbeams, sea mist, Mediterranean heat shimmer.
- **Technical**: Sharp textures on stone and fabric, 8K clarity, naturalistic rendering.

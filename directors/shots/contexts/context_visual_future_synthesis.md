# Visual Context: Future Synthesis (Year 2126+)
## The Era of Synthesis
- **Sentient Architecture**: Buildings as biological-synthetic hybrids (Zaha Hadid/Calatrava style). Curvilinear, biomimetic forms (bone, wind, water), "skin" that breathes.
- **Urban Forests**: Cities indistinguishable from lush jungles. Pearl-white ceramic towers rising from dense canopy.
- **Atmosphere**: Crystal clear air with shimmering "weather-drones" appearing as sparkling dust motes. Bioluminescent flora providing a gentle nighttime glow.

## Transhumanist Tech
- **Medical**: Genomic rejuvenation (youthful elders), neural lace (subtle light patterns under the skin at temples/neck), medical nanobots appearing as golden mist.
- **Synthesizers**: Molecular feeders that assemble objects/food from shimmering light fields.
- **Transportation**: Silent anti-gravity pods (Atmospheric Pods) gliding through sky-lanes. Orbital elevators (column-of-light spires reaching space).

## Off-World Colonization
- **Mars**: Terraformed with young blue oceans, red sand, and silver domed cities.
- **The Moon**: Glowing hub of science and tourism.
- **Habitats**: O'Neill Cylinders with perfect internal ecosystems.

## Style Guardrails
- **Aesthetic**: White, gold, and iridescent polymers. Translucent materials.
- **STRICTLY NO**: Grime, rust, sharp industrial edges, exposed wires, or dystopian decay.

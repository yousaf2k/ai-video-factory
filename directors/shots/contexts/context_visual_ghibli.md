# Context: Ghibli Anime World
## World Building
- **Environment**: Environments should feel magical, nostalgic, and deeply connected to nature. Overgrown ruins, cozy cluttered interiors (like bakeries or workshops), vast rolling meadows, and towering cumulus clouds are staples.
- **Scale**: Emphasize the awe-inspiring scale of nature, flying contraptions, or ancient structures compared to the human characters.
- **Atmosphere**: Peaceful, nostalgic, and slightly magical. Use atmospheric perspective with soft misty backgrounds, drifting pollen, or glowing fireflies.

## Subject Integration
- **Interaction**: Characters should interact gently with nature. Food should look incredibly appetizing and detailed.
- **Emotion**: The environment should evoke a sense of wonder, tranquility, or nostalgic longing (mono no aware).

# Visual Context: Indus Valley Civilization (c. 3300-1300 BCE)
## Architecture & Urbanism (Pristine, Not Ruins)
- **City Grid**: Perfectly north-south, east-west aligned streets, grid-based layout.
- **Citadel & Lower City**: Raised western citadel for important buildings; lower city for residential/commercial.
- **Advanced Drainage**: Covered baked brick drains running along streets with inspection hatches/soak-pits.
- **Great Bath**: Mohenjo-daro's waterproof brick pool (bitumen sealed), surrounded by columned portico.
- **Granaries**: Massive brick storage buildings with ventilation air ducts and earthen ramps.
- **Residential**: Multi-story courtyard houses, private wells in courtyards, indoor bathrooms.
- **Standardized Bricks**: 7:14:28 ratio, sun-dried and baked bricks, sharp uniform edges.

## Clothing & Adornment
- **Textiles**: Pioneers of woven cotton; lightweight, breathable linen/cotton.
- **Men**: Loincloth/Dhoti, long shawls draped over left shoulder (right shoulder bare), simple turbans.
- **Women**: Knee-length skirts, sashes, shawls; hair in stylized braids/buns with ribbons.
- **Jewelry**: Elaborate gold necklaces, multiple bangles on both arms (from wrist to shoulder), nose rings, anklets.
- **Materials**: Carnelian (orange-red), Lapis Lazuli (deep blue), Steatite, Gold, Copper.

## Material Culture & Technology
- **Seals**: Square steatite seals with animal motifs (Unicorn bull, elephant, tiger) and Indus script.
- **Pottery**: Red ware pottery with black geometric or floral designs, wheel-turned, high quality.
- **Tools**: Polished copper mirrors, copper chisels/axes, precise stone weights.
- **Toys**: Terracotta ox carts, whistles, figurines.

## Agriculture & Animals
- **Draft Animals**: Humped zebu cattle, water buffalo. **STRICTLY NO HORSES**.
- **Crops**: Wheat, barley, cotton, sesame, dates.

## Environmental & Lighting
- **Atmosphere**: Hot, semi-arid Indus River valley; intense sunlight, dust particles in sunbeams.
- **Colors**: Terracotta red, sun-dried mud brown, unbleached cotton white, indigo blue, madder red, turquoise.
- **Lighting**: Harsh midday contrasts, golden hour warmth on brickwork, deep shadows in narrow alleys.

## Visual Guardrails
- **Show Living Cities**: Freshly plastered walls, gleaming brickwork, fully functional cities.
- **NO Ruins**: No broken walls, no erosion, no archaeological decay.

# Context: Indus Valley Civilization (c. 3300-1300 BCE)

## Era Overview
One of the world's three earliest urban civilizations, known for its extraordinary urban planning, standardized weights/measures, and sophisticated sanitation systems. It flourished in the basins of the Indus River at the same time as the Pyramids of Giza.

## Historical Facts & Timeline

### Periods of Development
- **Early Phase (3300-2600 BCE)**: Transition from village life to early urbanism (e.g., Mehrgarh, Early Harappan pottery).
- **Mature Phase (2600-1900 BCE)**: The peak of the civilization. Major cities like Mohenjo-Daro and Harappa are fully functional.
- **Late Phase (1900-1300 BCE)**: Gradual decline, abandonment of large cities, and migration toward the Ganges plain.

### Urban Engineering Marvels
- **Grid Layout**: Cities planned on precise north-south, east-west grids with cardinal alignment.
- **Standardized Bricks**: Baked bricks in a consistent 4:2:1 ratio (Length:Width:Height) used across all major sites.
- **Sanitation**: Covered brick drains large enough for a person to walk through, indoor bathrooms with flush toilets in private homes.
- **Public Structures**: The Great Bath at Mohenjo-Daro (waterproofed with bitumen) and massive granaries/citadels.
- **Dholavira**: Sophisticated water reservoirs and stone architecture.

### Trade & Economy (The Meluhha Connection)
- **Mesopotamia**: Identified as "Meluhha" in Sumerian records; traded carnelian, lapis lazuli, ivory, and cotton.
- **Weights & Measures**: Highly accurate, standardized binary systems for trade.
- **Sea Trade**: Lothal port featuring a massive dockyard for maritime commerce.

### The Great Mystery: The Script
- **Undeciphered**: Over 400-500 distinct signs found on steatite seals.
- **Seals**: Depicting animals (Unicorn, Bull, Elephant, Tiger) and the "Pashupati" figure.
- **Lost Voice**: Lack of deciphered writing means their myths, kings, and laws remain silent.

### The Decline (c. 1900 BCE)
- **Climate Change**: Weakening monsoons and shifts in the Ghaggar-Hakra (Sarasvati) river system.
- **Gradual Transformation**: Not a violent conquest, but a slow abandonment driven by water scarcity.

## High-Fidelity Sensory Details
- **Visuals**: Pristine red-brick cities with sharp edges, white cotton robes, elaborate bead jewelry (carnelian/gold), humped cattle in the streets.
- **Sounds**: The sound of bow drills in bead workshops, the splash of water in private courtyards, the bustle of the Forum markets.
- **Smells**: Fried flatbreads and lentils, fresh mud plaster, the earthen scent of active irrigation channels.

## Authentic Vocabulary
- *Mohenjo-Daro* (City), *Harappa* (City), *Steatite* (Seal Material), *Meluhha* (Trade Name), *Great Bath* (Structure).

## Anachronism Guardrails
- ❌ No Iron tools (This is a Bronze/Copper age culture).
- ❌ No Horses or wheeled war chariots (These arrived ~1500 BCE).
- ❌ No Hindu/Vedic imagery (Pre-Vedic; Shiva/Vishnu concepts are speculative here).
- ❌ No Palaces or Temples (No massive royal monuments have been found).
- ❌ No Sanskrit (The language is unknown).
- ❌ No Ruins (When writing journeys, show the cities at their peak).

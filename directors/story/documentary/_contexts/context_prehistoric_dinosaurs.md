# Context: Prehistoric Dinosaurs (252-66 million years ago)

## Era Overview
The Age of Reptiles, spanning the Mesozoic Era. A world of tectonic shifts, changing climates, and the evolution of the most magnificent land animals to ever exist.

## Historical Facts & Timeline

### Triassic Period (252-201 MYA)
- **First Dinosaurs**: Small, early bipedal species (e.g., Coelophysis).
- **Pangaea**: The supercontinent remains largely intact; arid and harsh environments.
- **Archosaurs**: The rise of crocodile-like reptiles alongside the first dinosaurs.

### Jurassic Period (201-145 MYA)
- **Giant Sauropods**: The age of Brachiosaurus and Diplodocus; lush, fern-filled forests.
- **Predators**: Allosaurus and Ceratosaurus dominate the hunting grounds.
- **Birds**: The first feathered species (Archaeopteryx) begin to appear.

### Cretaceous Period (145-66 MYA)
- **Apex Predators**: Tyrannosaurus Rex and Spinosaurus reaches peak power.
- **Defense**: Evolution of Ceratopsians (Triceratops) and Ankylosaurs (armored tanks).
- **Flowering Plants**: The landscape changes with the first flowers and diverse pollinators.
- **Extinction**: Ends with the K-Pg boundary (Asteroid impact/volcanism).

## High-Fidelity Sensory Details
- **Visuals**: Gleaming scales, vibrant feathers (on some species), massive towering canopy trees, glowing sunset over Pangaea, the ground shaking with 50-ton footsteps.
- **Sounds**: Low-frequency rumbles of sauropods, the piercing screech of pterosaurs, the rhythmic thud of a hunt.
- **Smells**: Thick humidity, decaying vegetation, the scent of crushed ferns, the metallic smell of water in ancient swamps.

## Authentic Vocabulary
- *Theropod* (Bipedal Carnivore), *Sauropod* (Long-necked Herbivore), *Crest/Frill* (Anatomical Features), *Pangaea* (Supercontinent), *Mesozoic* (Era Name).

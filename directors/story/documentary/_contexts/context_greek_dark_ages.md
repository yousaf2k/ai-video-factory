# Context: Greek Dark Ages (c. 1100-800 BCE)

## Era Overview
A mysterious period of collapse following the fall of the Mycenaean civilization. A time of population decline, loss of writing (Linear B), and the slow emergence of the iron-working culture that preceded the Archaic Age.

## Historical Facts & Timeline

### The Collapse (c. 1100-1050 BCE)
- **Palace Destructions**: Burning and abandonment of Pylos, Mycenae, and Tiryns.
- **Population Decline**: 50-75% reduction in population.
- **Loss of Literacy**: Disappearance of Linear B script.
- **Dorian Migration**: Theories of northern tribes moving south into the Peloponnese.

### The Low Point (1050-900 BCE)
- **Proto-Geometric Pottery**: Simple patterns showing reduced technical skills.
- **Small Settlements**: Villages of 10-50 people living among ruins.
- **Oral Traditions**: Preservation of history through singers and poets.

### Recovery (900-800 BCE)
- **Iron Technology**: Transition from bronze to more accessible iron.
- **Geometric Sophistication**: Increasingly complex pottery patterns.
- **Trade Revival**: Renewed contact with Cyprus, Phoenicia, and the Near East.
- **Lefkandi**: Elite burials revealing growing wealth and social hierarchy.

## Cultural Knowledge & Beliefs
- **Basileus Leadership**: Chieftains ruling through personal loyalty.
- **Warrior Ethos**: Focus on courage, raids, and iron weapons.
- **Memory & Myth**: Ancestor worship at Mycenaean tombs ("Hero Cults").
- **Homeric Precursors**: The oral stories that later became the Iliad and Odyssey.

## High-Fidelity Sensory Details
- **Visuals**: Ruins of Mycenaean palaces overrun by weeds, mud-brick huts with thatched roofs, simple woolen garments, gray iron swords.
- **Sounds**: The crackle of campfires, the recitation of epic poems by singers, the rhythmic pounding of iron on an anvil.
- **Smells**: Smoke from simple hearths, the earthen smell of unpaved village roads, the dampness of hillside settlements.

## Authentic Vocabulary
- *Basileus* (Leader), *Proto-Geometric* (Pottery Style), *Iron Age* (Metallurgy Shift), *Heroön* (Hero Grave), *Geometric* (Artistic Motif).

## Anachronism Guardrails
- ❌ No Classical Greek temples (No marble, no Ionic/Doric orders).
- ❌ No Organized hoplite phalanxes (This is individual warrior combat).
- ❌ No Written records (Linear B is lost; the alphabet hasn't arrived).
- ❌ No Black-figure or Red-figure pottery (Only simple geometric lines).

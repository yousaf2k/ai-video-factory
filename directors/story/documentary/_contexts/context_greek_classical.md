# Context: Greek Classical Period (c. 490-323 BCE)

## Era Overview
The "Golden Age" of Greece, marked by the peak of Athenian democracy, the height of philosophical inquiry, and the expansion of Greek influence under Alexander the Great.

## Historical Facts & Timeline

### The Persian Wars (490-479 BCE)
- **Marathon (490 BCE)**: Athenian victory over Darius' invasion force.
- **Thermopylae (480 BCE)**: Spartan sacrifice under Leonidas against Xerxes.
- **Salamis (480 BCE)**: Decisive Athenian naval victory.
- **Plataea (479 BCE)**: Final defeat of the Persian land invasion.

### The Golden Age of Pericles (479-431 BCE)
- **Athenian Democracy**: Full development of citizen participation.
- **The Acropolis**: Construction of the Parthenon (447-432 BCE), Propylaea, and Erechtheion.
- **Philosophical Roots**: Anaxagoras and the early Sophists.
- **Delian League**: Transformation from a defensive alliance to an Athenian empire.

### The Peloponnesian War (431-404 BCE)
- **Archidamian War**: Spartan invasions and the Athenian plague (430 BCE).
- **Sicilian Expedition (415-413 BCE)**: Catastrophic Athenian defeat.
- **Fall of Athens**: Lysander tears down the Long Walls; rule of the Thirty Tyrants.

### Alexander the Great (336-323 BCE)
- **Conquests**: Crossing the Hellespont, battles of Issus and Gaugamela.
- **Empire**: Reaching Egypt (founding Alexandria) and the Indus River.
- **Death**: 323 BCE in Babylon, ending the Classical era.

## Cultural & Philosophical Knowledge
- **Philosophy**: Socrates (dialectic), Plato (The Academy, Forms), Aristotle (Lyceum).
- **Drama**: Sophocles (Oedipus), Euripides (Medea), Aristophanes (Comedy).
- **History**: Herodotus (The Histories), Thucydides (Peloponnesian War).
- **Art**: Phidias (Parthenon sculptures), realistic but idealized human forms.

## High-Fidelity Sensory Details
- **Visuals**: Gleaming Pentelic marble, vibrant painted statues (polychromy), the crowded Pnyx hill, triremes in the Piraeus harbor.
- **Sounds**: The rhythmic chant of rowers, the clamor of the Agora, the resonance of tragedy in the Theater of Dionysus.
- **Smells**: Incense and burnt offerings at altars, the salt air of the Aegean, the sweat of the palaestra.

## Authentic Vocabulary
- *Himation/Peplos* (Clothing), *Basileus* (King/Leader), *Hoplite* (Soldier), *Trireme* (Ship), *Megaron* (Great Hall), *Agora* (Market/Assembly).

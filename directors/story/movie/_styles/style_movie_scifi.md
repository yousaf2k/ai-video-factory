# Style: Sci-Fi Movie Narrative
## Narrative Tone
- **Intensity**: Futuristic, speculative, and awe-inspiring.
- **Pacing**: Varies from slow discovery to intense technological conflict.
- **Impact**: Mind-bending concepts, advanced technology, space exploration.

## Emotional Beats
- **Beat 1**: Discovery of the unknown.
- **Beat 2**: Technological or cosmic dread.
- **Beat 3**: Humanity's place in the universe.

## Scripting Guidelines
- **Action Beats**: Use analytical verbs: 'calculates', 'warps', 'synthesizes'.
- **Narration**: Clinical, awe-struck, or deeply philosophical.
- **Dynamics**: Balance cold hard science with human emotion.

# Style: Drama Movie Narrative
## Narrative Tone
- **Intensity**: Grounded, character-driven, and serious.
- **Pacing**: Methodical, allowing room for dialogue and silence to breathe.
- **Impact**: Emotional devastation, hard truths, moral conflicts.

## Emotional Beats
- **Beat 1**: The burden of the past.
- **Beat 2**: The confrontation of deep-seated issues.
- **Beat 3**: Acceptance, tragedy, or hard-fought growth.

## Scripting Guidelines
- **Action Beats**: Use heavy verbs: 'weeps', 'stares', 'confesses'.
- **Narration**: Introspective, honest, and somber.
- **Dynamics**: Quiet, internal struggles boiling over into vocal conflicts.

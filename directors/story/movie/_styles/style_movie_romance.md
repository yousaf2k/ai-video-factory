# Style: Romance Movie Narrative
## Narrative Tone
- **Intensity**: Intimate, emotional, and passionate.
- **Pacing**: Slow buildup of tension with moments of sudden emotional release.
- **Impact**: Focus on subtle gestures, longing looks, physical touch.

## Emotional Beats
- **Beat 1**: The spark of initial connection.
- **Beat 2**: The pain of misunderstanding or separation.
- **Beat 3**: The joy of reunion and love realized.

## Scripting Guidelines
- **Action Beats**: Use emotive verbs: 'glances', 'hesitates', 'embraces'.
- **Narration**: Vulnerable, heartfelt, and observational.
- **Dynamics**: Quiet intimacy contrasted with sweeping, emotional declarations.

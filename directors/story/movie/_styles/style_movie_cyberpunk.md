# Style: Cyberpunk Movie Narrative
## Narrative Tone
- **Intensity**: Gritty, neon-drenched, and dystopian.
- **Pacing**: Fast and frenetic, mirroring an overloaded digital world.
- **Impact**: Neon lights reflecting in puddles, sparking cybernetics, data streams.

## Emotional Beats
- **Beat 1**: The struggle of the underclass against megacorporations.
- **Beat 2**: The blurred line between human and machine.
- **Beat 3**: Hacking the mainframe or escaping the neon city.

## Scripting Guidelines
- **Action Beats**: Use synthetic verbs: 'glitches', 'jacks in', 'overloads'.
- **Narration**: Cynical, tech-heavy, and street-smart.
- **Dynamics**: High-tech gloss hiding low-life rot.

# Context: Action Movie Tropes
## High-Speed Chases
- **Vehicular Chaos**: Squealing tires, dodging traffic, leaping from gaps.
- **Urban Parkour**: Sprinting through rooftops, scaling fire escapes.
- **The Getaway**: Intense pursuit through narrow alleys.

## Tactical Combat
- **The Professional**: Precise, calculated strikes and defensive maneuvers.
- **The Brawl**: Gritty, desperate hand-to-hand fighting.
- **The Siege**: Defending a position against overwhelming numbers.

## Explosive Set Pieces
- **Environmental Hazard**: Crumbling bridges, collapsing buildings, forest fires.
- **The Ambush**: Sudden, surprising attacks on exposed characters.
- **Final Confrontation**: The high-stakes showdown in a dramatic location.

## Against All Odds
- **Infiltration**: Sneaking into a heavily guarded facility.
- **Escapology**: Characters trapped in dangerous situations needing a way out.
- **The Sacrifice**: Moments of heroism to save the mission or others.
